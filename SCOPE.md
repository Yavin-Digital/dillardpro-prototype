# Scope

In this prototype:

- Home, Cheat Sheet, Availabilities, Cities, Reviews, and Contact
- Home explainer video chapter
- One-section keyboard and scroll stepping, including full-screen phone chapters
- Official copy, town list, neighborhood groups, DFW area maps, floor plan, logo, Meg and Chris portrait, and a sample of the published Zillow reviews
- `about.html` redirects to home

Out of this prototype:

- An About page or a Chris and Meg biography
- People-served totals (100,000 and 300,000 disagree on the live site)
- The empty Wix placeholders (FIND, Money Back) and the coin gallery on `/money`
- Privacy policy and terms pages
- Per-property availability feeds
