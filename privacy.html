Privacy Policy

Draft for client and legal review. Last updated November 25, 2026.

This page is a draft for client and legal review. It is not legal advice and is not a final policy. It describes the information practices visible on this website prototype.

Who we are

Dillard Property Services provides apartment and property listings guidance in Greater Austin, DFW, Houston, and San Antonio, Texas.

The legal entity name and registered mailing address are not published on this website. Until those details are confirmed, use this placeholder: [Legal entity name and registered mailing address to be provided].

You can reach us through the contact options published on this site: info@dillardpropertyservices.com, text, and the office lines listed on the Contact page.

Information we collect

The Contact and Cheat Sheet forms on this site ask for:

  • First name and last name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Metro, and on the Cheat Sheet, area
  • Monthly budget, bedrooms, and move date (Cheat Sheet)
  • A short note about what you need, or other details you choose to provide

If you call or text a number published on this site, we receive the contact information and message content you send in that conversation, subject to your carrier’s services.

Prototype forms

On this review website, the Contact and Cheat Sheet forms are demonstrations. Submitting a form opens the Availabilities page in your browser. The entries are not sent to a server mailbox from this prototype.

Text messages

Where a form includes text-message consent language, that language applies to messages about rentals at the number you provide. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out. Text HELP to the published text number, or email info@dillardpropertyservices.com, for assistance.

How we intend to use information

When these forms are connected on a production website, the intended uses are to respond to a rental inquiry, prepare personalized listing recommendations, and communicate by text, phone, or email when you have provided those details and, for texts, your consent.

We may share information with a property or a service provider only as needed to fulfill a request you made. This draft does not claim a full list of vendors. Those details should be confirmed in legal review.

This draft does not state that personal information is sold. Whether any sharing would be treated as a “sale” or “share” under a particular privacy law should be confirmed in legal review.

Cookies and third-party services

This prototype does not include a first-party analytics or advertising tag. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The home page also loads animation scripts from a third-party CDN (jsDelivr / GSAP). Some images are served from Unsplash. Those providers may process your IP address or set their own cookies under their own policies.

Retention and security

Production retention periods and security measures are not specified on this website and should be confirmed in legal review. We intend to keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete information you provided, or to stop text messages, by using the contact options on this site or by replying STOP to a text. We will honor rights that apply to you under U.S. or Texas law, once the formal request process is completed in legal review.

Children

This website is intended for people seeking residential rentals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Texas and other laws

This service is directed at people looking for rentals in Texas. Additional notice or request procedures may be required under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act or other laws, depending on the confirmed legal entity and its obligations. Those procedures are left for legal review.

Changes

If this policy is adopted, we may update it from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top of the page will change when a revised version is posted.
